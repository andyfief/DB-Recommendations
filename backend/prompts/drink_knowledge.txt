AVAILABLE FLAVOR DIMENSIONS
Each flavor maps to a float value 0.0–1.0 representing its intensity in the drink.
A value of "default" means the standard proportion for that drink.

Common flavors:
- vanilla (sweet, creamy, classic)
- caramel (sweet, rich, buttery)
- chocolate (deep, rich, cocoa)
- white_chocolate (sweet, creamy, mild)
- mocha (chocolate + coffee combined)
- hazelnut (nutty, sweet)
- irish_cream (creamy, slightly boozy-flavored)
- coconut (tropical, sweet)
- strawberry (fruity, bright, sweet)
- peach (soft fruit, sweet)
- raspberry / red_raspberry (tart, bright berry)
- blue_raspberry (sweet-tart, candy-like)
- blackberry (dark berry, tart-sweet)
- mango (tropical, sweet, bright)
- pineapple (tropical, tart)
- watermelon (light, sweet, melon)
- passion_fruit (tropical, tart)
- orange (citrus, bright)
- lime (citrus, tart)
- lemon_concentrate (sour, citrus)
- peppermint (cool, minty)
- cinnamon (warm, spiced)
- salted_caramel (sweet + salty, caramel base)
- almond_orgeat (nutty, sweet)
- lavender (floral, light)
- honey (floral, sweet)
- cane_sugar (pure sweetness)
- tigers_blood (strawberry + coconut + watermelon blend)
- golden_eagle (caramel + butter + vanilla blend)
- electric_berry (mixed berry blend)

Sugar-free variants exist for many of the above (prefix: sugar_free_).
Example: sugar_free_vanilla, sugar_free_caramel, sugar_free_chocolate

AVAILABLE TOPPINGS
Each topping maps to a float representing quantity (1.0 = one standard serving, 2.0 = double, etc.)
Boolean-style toppings are present (1.0) or absent (0.0 / omitted).

- whip_cream (whipped cream on top)
- soft_top (sweet cream cold foam)
- sweet_cream (cold sweet cream, added to drink body)
- caramel_drizzle
- chocolate_drizzle
- white_chocolate_drizzle
- honey_drizzle
- cookie_butter_drizzle
- cinnamon_sprinks (cinnamon sprinkles on top)
- birthday_cake_sprinks
- raw_sugar_sprinks
- strawberry_poppin_boba (popping boba, strawberry flavor)
- blue_raspberry_poppin_boba
- strawberry_float (strawberry syrup float on top)
- coconut_float
- blackberry_float
- peach_float
- cherry_float
- pomegranate_float
- raspberry_float / red_raspberry_float
- lavender_float
- watermelon_float
- mango_float
- sour_shot (tart shot, goes in drink, makes it sour)

NOTES:
- Do not invent ingredients outside this list.
- When removing a flavor, omit it from the flavors dict entirely.
- When removing a topping, omit it from the toppings dict entirely.
